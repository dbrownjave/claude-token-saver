token-saver · Context pane

Design canvas for /tokens. Desktop frames are drawn at 860 px in the host UI font at one size. Terminal frames are 80 columns: a 1-column border, a 1-column pad and 76 content cells. Each frame is drawn from the same view model, so the strings table at the bottom matches what is drawn. Pink numbers mark annotations, read beside each pair.

Rationale. The first thing on screen is one number: tokens saved this session, with the estimate in your currency and the real spend beside it. That answers "is this paying for itself" before you read anything else. The context bar sits directly under it because the next question is "how close am I to my next mark". Its colour is the only alarm in the pane, and the tick, knob and "next" text all point to the same threshold. Expanding never moves those two lines: the ledger, cache, "where it goes" and actions are added below them, so your eye does not have to find the number again. The ledger is four lines, each with a bold right-aligned number and a dim label, so the column of numbers can be scanned in one look. The breakdown is cut to at most three rows, and only for categories that come with a command to run. System prompt, system tools and free space never get a row, because you cannot act on them, and they appear only as colour in the split bar. Cut entirely: the four limit cards (they live in /context), per-line dollars (dollars appear once in the headline and once on the month line), hit-rate history, and any legend. What an engineer gains: the answer and the next action are in the top two lines, both in collapsed and expanded, and in every billing mode.

0 · The context bar, up close

No knob. The end of the fill burns white-hot and its flames stream left on the wind. Each option is one file in design/fire/ following design/fire/README.md. Pick one here and every context bar on this canvas redraws with it. "Play turns" adds a reply's worth of tokens every 1.4 s so you can watch the head run right, change colour at each threshold, and reset on Clear.

M · Mid-session, iterations in Claude's theme

The expanded pane mid-session (API billing, 48%, cache warm), drawn in Claude's own colours: the desktop app's tokens on the left, the CLI theme the engine resolves on the right. Switch themes to check each variant in light and colour-blind themes too. The context bar is F1, its heat taken from the theme's threshold colour. Tokens: design/theme/claude-themes.json.

L · Ten layouts for M4, closed

Picked: L5, refined, on its own page: design/m4.html.

The context bar row is the same in every layout: F1, the percentage in the threshold colour. What moves is everything else: the three actions (Clear, Compact, Later on c, k, l), the two hints (cache dot, saved), and the chevron on e that replaces "More". esc appears once, in the title. Click any chevron to open that layout; the open view is M4's full pane. The theme buttons in section M apply here too.

Before the first reply, cache state and session totals are unknown. The month total and the startup load are known, and the startup load is the most useful thing to act on at this point.

Only these two lines change between billing modes. The layout is the same in all of them. No dollar is ever shown on a subscription.
