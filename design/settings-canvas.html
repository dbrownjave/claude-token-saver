What each direction costs

What this exposed, whichever direction wins

  1. The terminal shows 40% of the panel, or a narrow layout. Measured on the real CLI, opened as Settings opens it: under 110 columns the pane sits inline at a third of the height (76 × 6 on an 80 × 24 terminal, 96 × 14 on 100 × 50). From 110 columns it docks at 54 columns however wide the terminal is, even at 220, and narrow = cells < 60 gives every docked terminal the label-above-control layout. Asking rows: 17, columns: 72 in $.ui.open changed nothing, so a direction has to fit these, not ask for more.
  2. A refused save is off screen. settingsMsg draws on the last row. Press a row near the top of the 14-row inline pane and the save goes back with no word in view. Try "Refused save" in the bar: A's message lands on row 35, with 14 in view.
  3. Since 0.3.40 the panel contradicts the pane on a subscription. "Prompt cache TTL is 1 hour" reads Off while the cache really is one hour. Keep-warm reads On while the pane says off (not API billing). Pings and Input price change nothing on a plan. B to F say what is true for the plan and relabel the row "Treat the cache as 1 hour" (the plugin.json title would follow); A is left as it ships.
  4. The terminal's primary Button is bold periwinkle, and every bracketed Button is bold. Captured: #b1b9f9 dark, #5769f7 light. A Button takes no colour prop, and [ label ] is always bold. A plain Button is regular weight and can hold a Text, so a plain Button holding orange Text is pressable, orange and not bold. The real CLI drew that and Enter pressed it. C to G use this or orange Text for the active value.
  5. Tab into a Select opens it in place on the terminal. Closed it is one row, Auto ▾. Focused, its options take a row each and push the rows below down; Pings would add 11 rows to a 14-row pane. The 0.3.37 attempt kept −/+ on the terminal, and this is why that was right.
  6. An overfull terminal row breaks words a letter at a time. Buttons wider than the row wrapped as Pl / ai / nD in the capture. Any new control row has to be measured at 40.
  7. A Settings redesign already exists, unmerged: claude/sad-lewin-9851f3 (1106e19, be5354c) carries a different 0.3.37 with cards and a More button. Direction B is its layout, without the bold. It never reached 0.3.40.
